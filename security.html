Security& Privacy

This page describes the architecture and controls we build to, and the reasons for each. No system is risk-free; our aim is to reduce risk by design and be clear about what we do.

Ask a question

Principles

  • Minimise: collect only anchor measurements and a device identifier. No names, no phone contents.
  • Process locally: estimation can run on a gateway inside your network.
  • Protect in transit and at rest: encryption everywhere data moves or sits.
  • Limit access: each person sees only what their role requires.
  • Keep records: every access is logged.
Technology

What is used, and why.

Encryption in transit

TLS 1.3 between devices, gateways, servers and dashboards, with certificate validation. This prevents interception or modification of measurements on the network.

Encryption at rest

AES-256 for databases and backups, with keys held in a managed key store and rotated. A stolen disk does not expose location history.

Pseudonymous identifiers

Devices are tracked by rotating random IDs. The table linking an ID to a person or asset is stored separately with stricter access, so location records alone do not identify anyone.

On-device and edge processing

Raw measurements can be processed on a local gateway, and only the resulting position leaves the building if the customer chooses cloud analytics.

Role-based access control

Separate roles for administrators, operators and viewers, with multi-factor authentication for administrators and optional single sign-on.

Audit logging

Who viewed or exported which location data, and when. Logs are append-only and exportable to your own monitoring.

Signed anchors and gateways

Anchors and gateways authenticate with device certificates, which helps prevent a rogue device from injecting false measurements. Our sensor-trust checks add a second layer by flagging implausible readings.

Retention and deletion

Configurable retention, such as live-only or a fixed number of days, with automated deletion and deletion on request.

Secure development

Dependency scanning, code review, secrets kept out of source control, least-privilege service accounts and regular penetration testing before production releases.

Privacy and compliance

Respecting the people being located.

  • Designed to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: purpose limitation, consent where people carry tracked phones, and data-principal rights such as access and erasure.
  • For staff and patients, tracking is opt-in or limited to assets, and facilities can define zones where tracking is off.
  • Healthcare deployments can add stricter retention and role separation. Formal certifications such as ISO 27001 are a roadmap item, not a current claim.
  • The system is built to work with hidden-test-style validation, so accuracy claims are measured, not asserted.

What we cannot promise

No software is completely immune to attack. We reduce exposure through the controls above, test them regularly, and will notify customers promptly if an incident affects their data.
